# Side walk inspection

The previous v9 pasted legs had mismatched proportions and a cut skirt edge. Passing frames 2 and 4 were identical, making the cycle hard to read.

This folder keeps the intermediate imagegen repair, not the runtime-selected asset. The source was generated with the built-in image_gen tool. The assembly script extracts complete connected sprites and registers their crests; it does not paste new legs onto the old torso.

Visual acceptance must check both side directions at normal and slow speed, opposite lead legs in contact poses, consistent shoes and skirt joins. JavaScript tests only verify animation selection and movement, not the quality of the artwork.
